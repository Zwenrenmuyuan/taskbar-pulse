The module is embedded by res/app.rc. To replace it, replace AMDFamily17.bin
and rebuild with build.ps1. TaskbarMonitor only calls ioctl_read_smn to read
THM_TCON_CUR_TMP, while holding the shared Global\Access_PCI mutex.
The separately installed PawnIO driver verifies and runs the module.
